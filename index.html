About Us

Where Tradition Meets
Artistry

At Kanya's Pleating, we believe a perfectly draped saree is not just an outfit — it's an expression of grace, heritage, and identity. Founded with a deep love for the art of saree draping, we bring precision, patience, and personal care to every appointment.

Whether it's your wedding day, a festive celebration, or a creative photoshoot, our expert hands ensure you step out looking effortlessly elegant. Each pleat is crafted with intention, each pin placed with pride.

What We Offer

Our Services

Tailored draping for every occasion — from intimate gatherings to grand celebrations.

Bridal Draping

Your wedding day deserves perfection. We craft flawless bridal pleats with silk, Kanjivaram, and designer sarees — ensuring you look divine from every angle.

Wedding Events

Reception, mehendi, sangeet, or haldi — each function calls for a unique look. We dress bridesmaids and family members with consistent elegance.

Baby Showers

Celebrate new beginnings looking radiant. We help mothers-to-be feel comfortable and beautiful in traditional sarees styled with care and ease.

Festivals

Pongal, Navratri, Diwali, Onam — every festival is an opportunity to celebrate culture in a beautifully draped saree. Book us for the festive season.

Photoshoots

Camera-ready draping for portfolio shoots, maternity photography, and editorial looks. Every pleat is positioned for a picture-perfect result.

Custom Styling

Have a unique vision? We work with you to create bespoke draping styles — fusion, experimental, or regional variations — tailored to your personality.
